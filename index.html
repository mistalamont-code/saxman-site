About

The artist behind the horn.

Anthony Grayson — known on stage as Saxman — is a working saxophonist out of Erie, Pennsylvania. Versatile sound across jazz, R&B, gospel, contemporary worship, and modern pop.

He plays the cocktail hour like it's his living room. He turns a wedding ceremony into something nobody forgets. He's the live moment a corporate dinner didn't know it needed.

He's been playing long enough to read a room before the first note — and that's the difference between a saxophonist at your event and a saxophonist in your event.

Based in Erie, PA, available for travel. Travel rates apply outside 30 miles. Anthony brings his own sound system to every event — one less thing for you to think about.
